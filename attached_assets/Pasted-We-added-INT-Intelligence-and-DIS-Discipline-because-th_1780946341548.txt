We added INT (Intelligence) and DIS (Discipline) because the original four stats covered physical and wellness habits well, but they didn't reward two major areas of personal growth:
Learning
Consistency
Without INT and DIS, a user who reads every day or maintains perfect habit streaks wouldn't see that effort reflected in their Hunter.
INT — Intelligence
Productivity App Meaning
INT is earned from activities that improve knowledge and learning.
Examples:
Reading books
Studying
Online courses
Educational videos
Language learning
Skill development
The idea is:
"The more knowledge you gain in real life, the more intelligent your Hunter becomes."
Game Meaning
INT represents magical knowledge and mental power.
INT increases:
Magic damage
Ability power
Skill effectiveness
Spell scaling
Future possibilities:
Reduced skill cooldowns
Increased elemental damage
Better mana efficiency
Classes That Benefit Most
Sage
Primary stats:
INT
SEN
A Sage with high INT becomes a powerful spellcaster.
Example
Real Life:
Read 30 minutes
+10 INT XP
Game:
INT 20 → 21

Fireball Damage
120 → 128
DIS — Discipline
Productivity App Meaning
DIS is earned through consistency.
Not from what you do.
From how consistently you do it.
Examples:
Daily streaks
Habit completion rate
Weekly consistency
Consecutive productive days
Following routines
The idea is:
"Discipline is the ability to show up repeatedly."
Game Meaning
DIS represents efficiency, endurance, and progression.
We discussed making DIS one of the most unique stats because most RPGs don't have anything like it.
DIS affects:
Dungeon Energy
Resource efficiency
Bonus rewards
Progression speed
Possible future bonuses:
More daily dungeon entries
Increased gold gain
Increased XP gain
Better loot chance
Reduced energy costs
Classes That Benefit Most
Warden
Primary stats:
DIS
VIT
The Warden isn't the strongest damage dealer.
Instead:
Stays active longer
Earns more resources
Progresses efficiently
Example
Real Life:
7-day habit streak
+15 DIS XP
Game:
DIS 15 → 16

Dungeon Energy
100 → 105

Gold Bonus
+5%
Why These Two Stats Matter
Without them:
Real Life Activity	Stat
Exercise	STR
Stretching	AGI
Meditation	SEN
Sleep	VIT
Reading	?
Consistency	?
There would be gaps.
With INT and DIS:
Real Life Activity	Stat
Exercise	STR
Stretching	AGI
Meditation	SEN
Sleep	VIT
Reading / Learning	INT
Consistency / Streaks	DIS
Now every major self-improvement category has a corresponding Hunter stat.
